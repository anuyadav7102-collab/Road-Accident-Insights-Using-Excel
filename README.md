# Road Accident Data Analysis(Interactive DashBoard)
This project focuses on analyzing road accident data for the years 2021 and 2022 using Excel. The goal is to identify key accident trends, severity distribution, vehicle types involved, and high-risk areas. The final output is an interactive Excel dashboard that helps stakeholders understand accident patterns and improve road safety.
- <a href="https://github.com/anuyadav7102-collab/Road-Accident-Insights-Using-Excel/blob/my-new-branch/Dashboard/Road%20Accident%20Data.xlsx">Dataset</a>
# Question (KPIS)
🔶 Primary KPIs
Total Casualties recorded across all accident categories.
Casualty Breakdown by Severity (Fatal, Serious, Slight) with percentage contribution.
Maximum Casualties by Vehicle Type to identify the most affected category.
<br>
🔷 Secondary KPIs
Casualties by Vehicle Type (e.g., two-wheelers, cars, trucks, buses, etc.).
Casualties by Road Type (Highway, Urban Roads, Rural Roads, etc.).
Distribution of Casualties by Road Surface (Dry, Wet, Snowy, etc.).
📊 Trend Analysis
Monthly trend comparison of casualties for Current Year (CY) vs. Previous Year (PY).
Accident frequency pattern by Day vs. Night.
📍 Location & Relation Insights
Relationship between casualties by area/location (zones, regions, cities).
Impact of road and environmental conditions on total casualties.
✔️ Purpose of the Dashboard
To provide a clear, interactive, and actionable view of road accident trends, helping stakeholders identify high-risk categories, understand causes, and support data-driven decision-making for improving road safety.

# Proccess
Cleaned the accident dataset by removing duplicates, fixing missing values, and standardizing categories to ensure accurate KPI calculation
Prepared the data by creating fields like Total Casualties, Month, and Day/Night, and grouping it by vehicle type, road type, and severity for dashboard use.
Analyzed total casualties, severity distribution, vehicle-type impact, monthly CY vs PY trends, and day/night accident patterns as per project requirements
Visualized insights using an interactive Excel dashboard with KPIs, bar charts, donut charts, and trend lines based on the provided requirements.
# Project Insight 
Slight and Serious injuries formed the majority, while Fatal casualties were comparatively lower but still significant.
Day vs Night comparison revealed that most accidents occurred during the daytime, but night-time accidents had relatively higher severity.
Current Year (CY) vs Previous Year (PY) comparison highlighted noticeable changes in accident patterns, helping track improvement or worsening conditions.
